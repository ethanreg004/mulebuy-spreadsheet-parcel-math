Quick FAQ

Quick parcel-math questions

Which divisor is correct?

None universally; record the one your carrier context uses and cite it.

Why compare to actual weight?

Carriers typically charge on the larger of the two; the comparison flags which applies.

When is an estimate dead?

At the order step, or when any input dimension changes.
