Parcel Math Notes FAQ

Answers keep arithmetic honest about being arithmetic.

Follow the method
Input questions Result questions Requote questions

Input questions

How precise should dimensions be?
As precise as your measuring allows; record units and rounding together.
Can I use listing dimensions?
Yes as a first pass, labeled as listing-sourced until measured.
What about packaging?
Note whether inputs include packaging; the note says which.

Result questions

Is a bigger volumetric weight bad?
It is information; the note flags which weight governs, not whether it is bad.
Can estimates be compared across rows?
Yes when divisors and dates match; otherwise compare inputs, not results.
Do estimates predict taxes?
No; duty questions are separate and belong to the platform's customs pages.

Requote questions

What triggers a requote?
An order step, a changed input, or a stale calculation date.
Where does the quote go?
On the row as a dated observation from the live quoting flow.
Does the estimate get deleted?
No; it is superseded and stays for history.

Math hygiene

Estimates that admit being estimates

A dated estimate with named inputs helps plan; an undated number posing as a quote misleads exactly when money is involved.

Observed beats assumed

Answers point to what a dated observation can support.

Gaps stay visible

Unanswered parts of a question stay named instead of papered over.

Decisions stay dated

Every recheck carries the date that makes it retraceable.

Carry unanswered parts into the next live check

A static answer should never disguise a changing platform detail.

Real quotes and routes belong to the Mulebuy shipping guide, not a saved estimate.
